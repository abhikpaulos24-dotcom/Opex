# SOP-MNT-014 Spindle bearing inspection and replacement

## Purpose
Inspect a spindle that shows bearing warning signs (vibration rising, spindle running hot,
noise, chatter marks on parts) and replace the bearing set when needed.

## Safety procedure
1. Apply Lockout-Tagout (SOP-MNT-009) before opening any spindle cover.
2. Let the spindle cool for at least 30 minutes; housings above 60 °C cause burns.
3. Wear cut-resistant gloves and safety glasses. Use the spindle lifting fixture for cartridges above 20 kg.

## Inspection steps
1. With the machine still running (before LOTO), record vibration on the spindle nose at 3 speeds (50%, 75%, 100% of programmed rpm) using the portable analyser.
2. Record spindle housing temperature after 15 minutes at the programmed speed.
3. Listen for grinding or rumbling at low speed.
4. After LOTO, check radial runout at the spindle nose with a dial indicator: rotate by hand, note total indicator reading.
5. Check axial play by pushing and pulling the spindle with a lever and indicator.
6. Inspect the lubrication: oil-air flow at the spindle (or grease condition on grease-packed spindles). Lack of lubricant is the most common cause of fast bearing failure.

## Required tools
Portable vibration analyser, contact thermometer, dial indicator with magnetic base, torque wrench set, bearing heater, spindle lifting fixture, lint-free cloth.

## Expected readings (healthy)
- Vibration: below 2.8 mm/s RMS (ISO 10816 zone A/B for rigid medium machines). 2.8–4.5 mm/s = plan a repair; above 4.5 mm/s = stop and repair.
- Housing temperature rise: less than 20 °C above ambient after 15 minutes.
- Radial runout: 3 µm or less at the spindle nose.
- Axial play: none measurable.

## Decision
- Lubrication found starved, bearings quiet after re-lubrication and vibration back below 2.8 mm/s: restore lubrication, monitor for 8 hours, schedule bearing check at the next maintenance window.
- Runout above 5 µm, grinding noise, or vibration still above 4.5 mm/s: replace the bearing set.

## Replacement procedure
1. Remove the drawbar and the front cover; mark the orientation of every part.
2. Withdraw the spindle cartridge using the fixture. Do not hammer on the spindle.
3. Press off the old bearings; inspect the races for pitting or spalling and photograph them for the incident report.
4. Heat new bearings to 80 °C on the induction heater; fit them in the matched set orientation (marks aligned).
5. Set preload to the value on the spindle data plate; tighten the locknut to the specified torque.
6. Refit the cartridge, run in: 10 minutes each at 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% speed, checking temperature at each step.
7. Re-check runout (3 µm or less) and vibration (below 2.8 mm/s), then release to production with first-off inspection (SOP-QA-011).

## Typical duration
Planned replacement with parts on the shelf: 5–7 hours. After a breakdown: 12–36 hours (damage to the spindle shaft, waiting for parts).
