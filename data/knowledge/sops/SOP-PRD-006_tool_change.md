# SOP-PRD-006 Tool change and tool-life management

## Purpose
Change cutting tools before they fail. A broken tool scraps the part, can damage the fixture, and stops the machine.

## When to change the tool
- When the tool-life counter reaches its limit, OR
- Earlier, when OpEx warns of overstrain or tool-wear failure risk (high cutting torque together with high tool wear), OR
- When surface finish or size starts drifting, or the cut sounds different.
Hard material batches raise cutting torque: expect to change tools 15–25% earlier on these batches.

## Safety
Use the tool-change position and feed hold; never reach into the work zone with the spindle turning. Wear cut-resistant gloves when handling inserts.

## Steps
1. Finish the current part or stop at a safe block; retract to the tool-change position.
2. Replace the insert or the tool; clean the pocket and seat.
3. Measure the new tool on the presetter or with the machine probe and update the offset.
4. Reset the tool-life counter.
5. Run the first part with feed override at 80% and check it (SOP-QA-011).

## Typical duration
5–8 minutes.
