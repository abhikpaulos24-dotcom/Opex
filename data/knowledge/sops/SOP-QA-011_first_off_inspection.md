# SOP-QA-011 First-off and in-process inspection after maintenance

## Purpose
Confirm a machine makes good parts after any repair, tool change, or alarm.

## Steps
1. Run one part at normal speed after the run-in.
2. Measure all critical dimensions on the control plan (bore diameters, positions, surface finish) with the CMM or calibrated gauges.
3. If all are within tolerance, release the machine and record the result.
4. If any dimension is out of tolerance, quarantine the part, inform the shift engineer, and do not restart production until the cause is found.
5. For 2 hours after a spindle or coolant repair, measure every 10th part for the critical dimensions.

## Quarantine
Parts made in the 30 minutes before a breakdown are held for 100% inspection.
