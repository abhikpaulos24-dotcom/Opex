# SOP-MNT-021 Coolant system troubleshooting

## Purpose
Restore coolant pressure and flow. Low coolant pressure makes parts run hot, causes size drift (thermal growth) and shortens tool life.

## Safety
Coolant is a skin irritant: wear nitrile gloves and glasses. Apply Lockout-Tagout (SOP-MNT-009) before opening the pump or filter housing.

## Inspection steps
1. Read coolant pressure at the machine gauge and compare with the normal value on the machine label (flood coolant typically 6–7.5 bar).
2. Check the tank level and the coolant concentration with a refractometer (target 6–8% for most semi-synthetics).
3. Check the filter differential pressure; a high difference means a clogged filter.
4. Listen to the pump: a whine or rattle suggests a worn impeller or cavitation.
5. For through-spindle coolant, check the rotary union for leaks.

## Expected readings
- Pressure within 10% of the label value.
- Filter differential below the limit printed on the housing.
- Concentration 6–8%.

## Fix
- Clogged filter: replace the filter element, clean the tank strainer (30–45 minutes).
- Low concentration or dirty coolant: top up or change coolant.
- Worn pump impeller: replace the pump (about 2 hours with a spare on the shelf).
- Rotary union leak: replace the seal kit.

## After the fix
Run 15 minutes, confirm pressure is back in range, and do a first-off size check (SOP-QA-011) because thermal drift may have affected the last parts.
