# SOP-INS-005 Vibration measurement and sensor calibration

## Purpose
Keep condition-monitoring sensors trustworthy. A drifting or failing sensor can hide a real fault or create a false alarm.

## Signs a sensor (not the machine) is the problem
- One sensor changes while related signals do not (vibration rising, but spindle temperature and motor power unchanged).
- Readings missing in gaps, or stuck at one value.
- The reading jumps when the cable is touched.

## Check and calibrate
1. Compare the fixed sensor with the portable analyser held at the same point; they should agree within 10%.
2. Inspect the cable and connector; re-seat and secure the cable away from moving parts.
3. Check the sensor mounting: magnetic bases must sit flat; stud-mounted sensors must be torqued to spec.
4. If readings still disagree, replace the sensor and record the calibration date in the asset register.

## Calibration interval
Every 12 months, or after any crash or spindle repair.

## Vibration severity guide (ISO 10816-3, rigid mounting, medium machines)
- Below 1.4 mm/s: new machine condition
- 1.4–2.8 mm/s: fine for long-term running
- 2.8–4.5 mm/s: limited running; plan a repair
- Above 4.5 mm/s: risk of damage; stop and repair
