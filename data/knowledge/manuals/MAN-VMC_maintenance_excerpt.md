# Machining centre maintenance manual - excerpt (generic, demo plant)

## Lubrication schedule
- Spindle bearings (oil-air): check oil-air unit level daily; the low-level alarm stops the spindle after 30 minutes.
- Linear guides and ball screws: automatic lubrication every 30 minutes; check reservoir weekly.
- Grease-packed spindles: no routine re-greasing; any temperature rise above 20 °C over ambient needs investigation.

## Preventive maintenance every 500 operating hours
Clean coolant tank strainer, check belt tension, check way-cover wipers, check door interlocks, record spindle vibration at 3 speeds, record axis backlash.

## Spindle limits
- Maximum continuous housing temperature: 55 °C.
- Vibration alarm (if fitted): warning at 4.5 mm/s, stop at 7.1 mm/s.
- Bearing preload: see spindle data plate. Never adjust preload without the spindle service tool.

## Common symptoms
| Symptom | Likely cause | First check |
|---|---|---|
| Spindle hot, vibration rising | Bearing wear or lubrication starvation | Oil-air flow, vibration spectrum |
| Speed drops under load | Drive belt slip | Belt tension, glazing |
| Parts oversize after 1 h | Thermal growth, low coolant | Coolant pressure, spindle temperature |
| Servo overvoltage alarm | Regeneration fault, loose terminal | Panel thermography, DC bus voltage |
| Chatter marks | Worn tool or bearing | Tool life, spindle runout |
